How blocks are found

A demand block is the last down candle before a bullish push that breaks the prior swing high. A supply block is the last up candle before a bearish push that breaks the prior swing low. The time shown is the open time of that last opposite-colour candle, which is the candle the zone is drawn from. A block is only listed once the pushing bar has closed. Push vol is the average volume of the pushing bars against the usual volume; quiet means 0.92x or less.

This page keeps only thin blocks (zone 1 ATR tall or less) where price then ran at least the chosen number of ATR away before first coming back. Push is measured from the zone edge to the furthest price reached before the first revisit. 4H aligned means an unbroken 4H block on the same side overlaps the zone. Both in-session and gap-formed blocks are included.

Fresh means price has not traded back into the zone since it formed. Revisited means price came back into it without closing through the far side. To zone is the percent from the last price to the nearest zone edge, negative when the zone is above price. Bars are from the SIP feed, about 15 minutes behind the market. Information only, not advice.
